# Models and coverage

Status: rewritten in plain words on 15 November 2026, every item checked against its source and today's code, and brought up to date on 18 November; the research is `research/models.md`, kept whole.

Plan: ✅ 9 done · 🚧 5 in progress · 📋 7 planned · ⏳ 1 waits for the owner · ⏸️ 2 parked (§4).

## 1. Goal and metric

jolty runs every analysis and every device a designer needs, and every open process kit natively, with models more exact than the references'.
Measured by the coverage matrix (`benchmarks.md` item 9), every cell a probe that ran.

## 2. Where we stand

- **30 native analog models** (31 in `Library::standard()` with `foundry_mosfet`, plus 2 logic models), BSIM4.5, the lossless transmission line and the capacitor of a charge among them since 15 November: resistors, capacitors and inductors with their coefficients and noise, coupled inductors, sources and their waveforms, controlled and behavioral sources, switches, diodes, the Gummel-Poon and VBIC 1.2 bipolar transistors, JFETs, MOSFETs of levels 1 to 3, VDMOS, BSIM3v3.3, BSIM4.5, memristors, a photocell, thermal elements and self-heating, and logic.
- **Every model is a kernel:** a pure function of its parameters, memory and local unknowns, with exact derivatives with respect to its voltages and every parameter, every one composed on dual numbers (`device-derivatives.md`).
    ngspice's sensitivities perturb each parameter by 1e-6; OSDI, ngspice's interface to compiled Verilog-A, passes no parameter derivatives at all.
- **The coverage matrix** (`benchmarks/coverage/coverage.json`, 65 rows): jolty offers 51 rows (50 agreed, 1 solved `vbic`, 0 disagreed after fixing `tline`'s transient probe to agree to 4.4e-16 of the scale, and 14 unsupported: `mos2`, `mos6`, `mos9`, `bsimsoi`, `hisim2`, `hicum`, `mesfet`, `jfet2`, `ltra`, `urc`, `xspice`, `osdi`, plus `lib` and `include` whose probes place `.lib`/`.include` inside a subcircuit even though top-level `.lib`/`.include` work).
    Of the 18 rows jolty lacked on 15 November, BSIM4, `.func` and raw files now agree, and the lossless transmission line is solved (items 8, 10, 19 and 22); ngspice lacks every DC solution, loop gain, transient sensitivities, a driven circuit's periodic steady state, two memristor models, the photocell, thermal networks and PSpice's `AKO` cards.
- **The open kits, nearly all native:** at their typical corners SKY130 has 251 of its 263 devices native, GF180MCU 65 of 72 and SG13G2 36 of 53, 14 more of whose are Verilog-A models (`docs/guide/foundry.md`, generated by `kits.inventory`).
- **The references:** ngspice 47 has 57 device directories and loads any Verilog-A model through OSDI; Xyce has 25 Verilog-A models, which ADMS turned into 856,000 checked-in lines of C++ its team patches by hand.
- **A model costs more than its equations:** beside its kernel, a large model takes 375–378 lines of `devices.py`, 218–287 of the SPICE reader and 63–108 of the exporter, and conflicts in four shared files; VBIC's commit touched 14 shared files.
- **The ports carry their sources' notices** (`9545e08`): `NOTICE`, BSIM's copyright and terms, ngspice's BSD notice, each ported file's module documentation.

## 3. Gaps

- The kits' last devices: SKY130's 12, GF180MCU's 7 and SG13G2's 17, BSIM4 4.6 and 4.62 and BSIM3 3.2 among what they need (items 8, 11 and 13).
- No CMC standard model in the library: four compile whole but wait for their QA tests, and FinFETs and SiGe are not compiled (items 12 to 15).
- No CMC QA tests (item 15) and no measured silicon (item 16); the common law suite is done (item 3, `6a5a8c0`).
- Only directional second derivatives in kernels: AC sensitivities are exact (`device-derivatives.md` 2b), while distortion and higher-order analyses wait for full second and higher derivatives (item 6).
- A model's facts are written in several files (item 4).

### ✅ 3. One law suite for every kernel

- **What:** physical laws every model must keep, tested over the whole library by enumeration: the charges summing to zero over the terminals; the n-channel and p-channel (npn and pnp) mirrors, bit for bit; the symmetry a model claims (Gummel's symmetry test to high order); current and its derivative continuous across region boundaries; finite values at any finite bias; beside the existing derivative checks.
- **Why:** every model, hand-written or compiled, held to the same physics; Xyce's division by zero in differentiated smoothing macros is the kind of fault it finds.
- **Done when:** every kernel passes, or each failure is stated as its source's, with evidence.
- **Result** (`6a5a8c0`): `crates/jolty-models/src/testing/laws.rs` holds every case of `cases::CASES` to conservation (within n²·ε), mirrors bit for bit, the swap symmetry each transistor claims (within rounding), continuity across region boundaries, and finite values at any finite bias.
    It found two faults, fixed: the photocell's smoothed light rounded to zero more than 67 lux below zero, giving NaN (`d5ade7d`); the Gummel-Poon and VBIC PNPs kept their excess phase in an NPN's sign, so they were not their NPNs' mirrors.
    Traced (18 November):
    - **BSIM3 `capmod 0` (1.4e-4) and small-`vds` precision:** three real numerical bugs fixed in `bsim3/channel.rs`, `bsim3/charges.rs`, `bsim4/channel.rs`, and `bsim4/charges.rs`: (1) `capmod_zero` branched on strict `channel.vbseff < 0.0`, sending `vbs == 0` into `(-channel.phis).plus(self.phi)` which inherited the left `vbseff` slope `0.9998515` (`1.48e-4` error, `1.85e-18 F` on `10u x 1u`) instead of `b3ld.c`'s smooth `1.0` derivative; (2) `smooth_positive + vbsc` cancelled ~12 digits near `vbs = 0`, causing branch chatter within `6e-12 V` (fixed with rationalized `smooth_floor` shared by BSIM3 and BSIM4); (3) `vdsat - (t1 + t2) * 0.5` cancelled at small `vds`, losing 5 to 14 digits (`37,886 ε` swap error at `[1e-9, 2, 0, -0.5]`, `1.51%` error on `Ids(1e-15)/1e-15`; fixed with two-sided rationalized `effective_drain` across all five BSIM3/BSIM4 sites, removing manual `vds == 0.0` hacks).
        Currents, charges and conductances now swap cleanly at `vds = 0` across all `capmod 0..=3`; remaining capacitance asymmetries (`capmod 0` strong inversion bulk/drain-source rows `14.1%` while gate row is `0.0`, forward body `19.2%`; `capmod 1` `1.47x`/`1.54x`; `capmod 2/3` `1.39e-4`/`6.86e-4` matching ngspice 47) are inherent Berkeley `b3ld.c` equations.
    - **MOSFET level 3 (1.7e-9):** not a level 3 bug, but the exact Meyer capacitance derivative kink (`DEVqmeyer`, `devsup.c` lines 698 to 720) shared identically by levels 1 and 3 when displacement currents flow (`w != 0`) inside `-phi < vgst <= 0`.
        With `w = 0` (DC/AC operating point), both levels swap exactly at `vds = 0`; with `w = 1e-9` on default `vto = 0, phi = 0.6`, the exact conductance jump is `|w| / phi * (1 - dVon/dvbs + 2 * sigma) = 1e-9 / 0.6 = 1.6667e-9` (identical for level 1 `tox = 2e-8` and level 3; full symmetric level 3 cards with `vto = 0.8 > phi` pass `assert_swaps` within rounding).
    Gummel's test at second order waits for second derivatives.
- **Lead.**
    **Source:** row 10; `research/quality.md` row 24.

### ⏸️ 5. Exact derivatives at the cost of hand-written ones

- **What:** derivatives computed by sparse forward differentiation at compile time, the way a compiler of Verilog-A would, instead of dual numbers carrying every partial at run time, where it pays.
- **Why, corrected** (17 November): the MOSFET kernels already compose small dual numbers at the natural width of each law (three for the channel, one per junction), so the zeros this item was to remove are not there for them; whether a MOSFET's evaluation costs more than ngspice's, and why, is unmeasured.
    OpenVAF's compiled HICUM outran ngspice's in one of two benchmarks.
- **Plan:** `device-derivatives.md` step 3, deferred until its measurement shows the derivatives' share of an evaluation worth the machinery.
- **Source:** row 12; with `speed.md` 5.

## 5. Decided

- On 14 November the owner left the list of what ngspice has aside; on 16 November he opened it, each item decided in its own entry above (§4).
- BSIM3 was ported before BSIM4; in hindsight BSIM4 serves more: SKY130's cards, the predictive ones and GF180MCU's.
- Tests use transistors of their own rather than vendors' published cards, which are their makers' copyright.
- VBIC's self-heating, read from a card's `RTH` and `CTH`, is checked against ngspice: the 29 K rise and the nets agree within 1e-6 (`test_a_heated_vbic_agrees`).
