# Models and coverage

Status: rewritten in plain language on 15 November 2026, checked against source documents and current code, and updated on 18 November.
Full background research remains archived in `research/models.md`.

Plan: ✅ 9 done · 🚧 5 in progress · 📋 7 planned · ⏳ 1 waits for the owner · ⏸️ 2 parked (§4).

## 1. Goal and metric

jolty runs every analysis and device a designer needs, simulates every open process kit natively, and matches or exceeds reference model accuracy.
Progress is measured by the coverage matrix (`benchmarks.md` item 9), where every cell represents an executed probe.

## 2. Where we stand

- **30 native analog models** (31 in `Library::standard()` with `foundry_mosfet`, plus 2 logic models), including BSIM4.5, the lossless transmission line, and the charge-based capacitor added since 15 November.
    Passive and source coverage includes resistors, capacitors, and inductors with coefficients and noise, coupled inductors, independent waveforms, controlled and behavioral sources, switches, thermal elements, self-heating, and logic.
    Semiconductor and specialty coverage includes diodes, Gummel-Poon and VBIC 1.2 bipolars, JFETs, MOSFET levels 1 to 3, VDMOS, BSIM3v3.3, BSIM4.5, memristors, and a photocell.
- **Every model is a kernel:** each kernel is a pure function of parameters, memory, and local unknowns, providing exact voltage and parameter derivatives via dual numbers (`device-derivatives.md`).
    ngspice sensitivities perturb each parameter by 1e-6, and OSDI (the ngspice interface to compiled Verilog-A) passes no parameter derivatives.
- **The coverage matrix** (`benchmarks/coverage/coverage.json`, 80 rows after v2): jolty records 62 agreed rows, 3 documented (`kit-sky130`, `kit-gf180mcu`, `kit-sg13g2`), 0 failed, 0 disagreed, and 15 unsupported.
    Recent agreed rows include `lib` and `include` inside subcircuits, `vbic` (8.1e-8 against exact coupled-KCL closed form), and `bsim3` (3.3e-16 in strong-inversion saturation).
    Additional agreed rows cover `corners`, `worst-case`, `switch-voltage`, `switch-current`, `logic`, `ic`, `nodeset`, `pdk-library`, and `four`.
    Unsupported rows are `disto`, `sp`, `trnoise`, `mos2`, `mos6`, `mos9`, `bsimsoi`, `hisim2`, `hicum`, `mesfet`, `jfet2`, `ltra`, `urc`, `xspice`, and `osdi`.
    Of the 18 rows jolty lacked on 15 November, BSIM4, `.func`, and raw files now agree, and the lossless transmission line is implemented (items 8, 10, 19, and 22).
    By comparison, ngspice lacks all-DC solutions, loop gain, transient sensitivities, driven periodic steady state, two memristor models, the photocell, thermal networks, and PSpice `AKO` cards.
- **Open process kits, nearly all native:** at typical corners, SKY130 has 251 of 263 devices native, GF180MCU has 65 of 72, and SG13G2 has 36 of 53 (`docs/guide/foundry.md`, generated by `kits.inventory`).
    Fourteen additional SG13G2 devices rely only on Verilog-A models.
- **Reference simulators:** ngspice 47 ships 57 device directories and loads Verilog-A models through OSDI; Xyce ships 25 Verilog-A models compiled by ADMS into 856,000 checked-in C++ lines patched by hand.
- **Model integration cost:** beyond the kernel equations, a large model requires 375 to 378 lines in `devices.py`, 218 to 287 in the SPICE reader, and 63 to 108 in the exporter.
    Adding a large model also causes merge conflicts in four shared files; the VBIC commit touched 14 shared files.
- **Ported model notices** (`9545e08`): `NOTICE`, BSIM copyright and terms, the ngspice BSD notice, and per-file module documentation carry upstream attributions.

## 3. Gaps

- Remaining open-kit devices: 12 in SKY130, 7 in GF180MCU, and 17 in SG13G2, needing BSIM4 4.6/4.62 and BSIM3 3.2 among other additions (items 8, 11, and 13).
- No CMC standard model in the library yet: four compile whole awaiting QA tests, while FinFET and SiGe models are not yet compiled (items 12 to 15).
- Missing CMC QA suite (item 15) and measured silicon benchmarks (item 16); the common physical-law suite is complete (item 3, `6a5a8c0`).
- Only directional second derivatives in kernels: AC sensitivities are exact (`device-derivatives.md` 2b), while distortion and higher-order analyses await full second and higher derivatives (item 6).
- Model metadata remains duplicated across several files (item 4).

### ✅ 3. One law suite for every kernel

- **What:** enumerate the entire model library to verify physical laws alongside existing derivative checks.
    Checked laws cover terminal charge conservation, bit-exact n/p-channel and NPN/PNP mirrors, claimed swap and Gummel symmetry, continuity of current and derivatives across regions, and finiteness at finite bias.
- **Why:** holding hand-written and compiled models to identical physics catches faults such as Xyce's division by zero in differentiated smoothing macros.
- **Done when:** every kernel passes, or any discrepancy is documented with evidence as upstream reference behavior.
- **Result** (`6a5a8c0`): `crates/jolty-models/src/testing/laws.rs` verifies every entry in `cases::CASES` for charge conservation (within n²·ε), bit-exact mirrors, claimed transistor swap symmetry (within rounding), region continuity, and finiteness.
    The suite uncovered and fixed two faults: photocell smoothed irradiance underflowed to zero below -67 lux causing NaN (`d5ade7d`), and Gummel-Poon and VBIC PNPs applied NPN excess-phase sign.
    Traced (18 November):
    - **BSIM3 `capmod 0` (1.4e-4) and small-`vds` precision:** three numerical bugs were fixed across `bsim3/channel.rs`, `bsim3/charges.rs`, `bsim4/channel.rs`, and `bsim4/charges.rs`.
        First, `capmod_zero` branched on strict `channel.vbseff < 0.0`, sending `vbs == 0` into `(-channel.phis).plus(self.phi)` with left `vbseff` slope `0.9998515` instead of `b3ld.c` slope `1.0`.
        The mismatched branch caused a `1.48e-4` error (`1.85e-18 F` on `10u x 1u`).
        Second, `smooth_positive + vbsc` canceled roughly 12 digits near `vbs = 0`, causing branch chatter within `6e-12 V` (fixed via rationalized `smooth_floor` shared by BSIM3 and BSIM4).
        Third, `vdsat - (t1 + t2) * 0.5` canceled at small `vds`, losing 5 to 14 digits (`37,886 ε` swap error at `[1e-9, 2, 0, -0.5]` and `1.51%` error on `Ids(1e-15)/1e-15`).
        Replacing all five BSIM3/BSIM4 sites with two-sided rationalized `effective_drain` fixed the cancellation and removed manual `vds == 0.0` special cases.
        Currents, charges, and conductances now swap cleanly at `vds = 0` across all `capmod 0..=3`.
        Remaining capacitance asymmetries are inherent to Berkeley `b3ld.c` equations.
        Measured asymmetries match ngspice 47: `capmod 0` strong-inversion bulk/drain-source rows (`14.1%`, gate row `0.0`) and forward body (`19.2%`), `capmod 1` (`1.47x`/`1.54x`), and `capmod 2/3` (`1.39e-4`/`6.86e-4`).
    - **MOSFET level 3 (1.7e-9):** the discrepancy reflects the Meyer capacitance derivative kink (`DEVqmeyer`, `devsup.c` lines 698 to 720), shared identically by levels 1 and 3 when `w != 0` inside `-phi < vgst <= 0`.
        With `w = 0` (DC/AC operating point), both levels swap exactly at `vds = 0`.
        With `w = 1e-9` on default `vto = 0, phi = 0.6`, the exact conductance jump is `|w| / phi * (1 - dVon/dvbs + 2 * sigma) = 1e-9 / 0.6 = 1.6667e-9`.
        The jump is identical for level 1 (`tox = 2e-8`) and level 3, while symmetric level 3 cards with `vto = 0.8 > phi` pass `assert_swaps` within rounding.
    Gummel's test at second order awaits second derivatives.
- **Lead.**
    **Source:** row 10; `research/quality.md` row 24.
    Item 3 replaces the duplicate entry previously in `quality.md`.

### 📋 4. Each model fact declared once alongside its kernel

- **What:** declare each model's parameters (SPICE name, dimension, default), terminal order, SPICE letter, node counts, and temperature convention once as data alongside the kernel.
    Python constructors, SPICE readers and exporters, reference pages, guide tables, and model-specific Python branches derive from or validate against that single declaration.
- **Why:** one parameter currently lives in six places across four files, a large model costs 650 to 770 lines outside the kernel, and `git grep '"vbic"'` hits across the Python layer.
- **Done when:** adding a kernel touches only the kernel file, tests, and guide section, adding a parameter touches one file, and `git grep '"vbic"'` hits only the constructor and tests.
- **Source:** row 13; `research/quality.md` rows 5 and 6.
    Item 4 replaces the `quality.md` items on single model-fact definitions and the model descriptor; the model-authoring guide (`quality.md` 19) follows once item 4 lands.

### ⏸️ 5. Exact derivatives at the cost of hand-written ones

- **What:** compute derivatives via compile-time sparse forward differentiation where beneficial, like a Verilog-A compiler, rather than carrying full dual-number partials at runtime.
- **Why, corrected** (17 November): MOSFET kernels already compose small dual numbers at the natural width of each law (three for the channel, one per junction), eliminating the zero partials originally targeted.
    Whether MOSFET evaluation still costs more than ngspice, and why, remains unmeasured; OpenVAF's compiled HICUM outran ngspice in one of two benchmarks.
- **Plan:** `device-derivatives.md` step 3, deferred until profiling shows derivative overhead justifies the extra machinery.
- **Source:** row 12; paired with `speed.md` 5.
